## Membership Tiers

### Bronze — N100,000 / year
- Free basic health check (registration required)
- Free tickets to LagosApps Concerts and Events
- Free Solar Audit

### Silver — N250,000 / year
- 1 free Car rental or 1 free Van/Bus rental
- Free Grocery Delivery (once a month)
- All Bronze membership benefits

### Gold — N500,000 / year
- Free 2-day stay at Mainland Wellness Centre
- At-home medical tests and checks (max 4 people)
- Free Grocery Delivery (2x limit per month)
- All Silver membership benefits
